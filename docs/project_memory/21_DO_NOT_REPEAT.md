# Do Not Repeat These Mistakes

1. Do not treat any 8-K as quarterly results without reading the exhibit.
2. Do not use period end as information time.
3. Do not choose examples after viewing outcomes.
4. Do not silently change evaluator formulas after freeze.
5. Do not let manual labels default to model predictions.
6. Do not count XBRL slices as independent facilities.
7. Do not use same-manager propagation as primary market information transfer.
8. Do not conflate borrower aliases with exact facility matches.
9. Do not count blank alias candidates as true negatives.
10. Do not publish raw vendor data or private mappings.
11. Do not let a direct filing URL leak protected outcomes to a blind reviewer.
12. Do not classify position disappearance after seeing numeric marks.
13. Do not build UI, agents, ML, NAV, or stock tests before the facility mechanism.
14. Do not reopen broad idea generation because a validation step is frustrating.
15. Do not call a point estimate a statistical guarantee.
