# Alternative Ideas and Why They Were Not Chosen

## Generic Coverage-Twilight Pre-Earnings

Strength:
- excellent product architecture;
- deep financial learning;
- PIT consensus asset.

Why not flagship:
- crowded;
- no unique free historical consensus;
- generic fundamentals are widely processed;
- exact stock reaction is noisy.

Retain:
- forecasting architecture, calibration, NO TRADE, live immutable journal.

## AI Reader Distortion / Model Monoculture

Strength:
- genuinely new market behavior;
- companies respond to machine readers;
- useful security/data-quality benchmark.

Why not flagship now:
- no proven bridge from shared LLM error to price formation;
- measurement requires multiple model families and stable prompts;
- natural distortion frequency unknown.

Retain:
- mechanism test and filing-sanitization/data-quality module.

## Regulatory Normalization Lag

Strength:
- new mandatory disclosures can create temporary standardization friction.

Why not standalone:
- vendors already normalize significant portions;
- some standards have too little history;
- comparability is incomplete;
- full segment P&L often cannot be reconstructed.

Retain:
- complexity and staleness features inside other forecasting systems.

## PowerQueue / Bankable MW

Strength:
- real recent large-load queue changes;
- documents contain deposits, site control, take-or-pay, ramp schedules.

Why not current flagship:
- state dockets fragmented;
- long economic horizon;
- difficult historical cancellation reconstruction;
- commercial data competition.

## EU Public Country-by-Country Reporting

Strength:
- fresh structured jurisdiction-level data;
- useful long-run proprietary asset.

Why not current flagship:
- little historical sample;
- live-first, not immediate backtest.

## Supplier-Finance Shadow Debt

Strength:
- financially important normalization of working capital and leverage.

Why not flagship:
- adjacent literature and commercial coverage already exist;
- better as a financial-model feature.

## Federal Contract Deobligation Nowcast

Strength:
- direct contract action to public-parent revenue/backlog impact.

Why not flagship:
- negative obligation does not always mean lost revenue;
- cancellation/re-award semantics are messy;
- existing contract-return literature.

## Prediction-Market Earnings Basis

Why not:
- limited contract coverage and liquidity;
- unclear scalable universe.

## Clinical-Trial Silent Delta

Why not:
- high binary risk and biotech-specific noise;
- difficult causal attribution.

## Silent XBRL Revision

Status:
- reserve mechanism, not current priority.
